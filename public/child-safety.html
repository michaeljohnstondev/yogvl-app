Child Safety Standards

The Yo by Big Vibe Studios | Last Updated: November 8, 2025

Zero Tolerance Policy: Big Vibe Studios has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users.

Age Requirements

The Yo is intended for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

  • Users must confirm they are 13+ during account creation
  • We immediately delete accounts found to belong to users under 13
  • Parents or guardians can report underage accounts to us

Child Sexual Abuse Prevention

We employ multiple safeguards to prevent child sexual abuse and exploitation:

Technical Safeguards

  • Content Moderation: User-generated content is monitored for inappropriate material
  • Reporting System: In-app reporting tools allow users to flag inappropriate content or behavior
  • Account Verification: Authentication requirements help verify user identity
  • Privacy Controls: Users can control who sees their profile and events
  • Blocking Features: Users can block and report other users

Policy Enforcement

  • Immediate removal of any CSAM content
  • Permanent ban of accounts involved in CSAE activities
  • Preservation of evidence for law enforcement
  • Cooperation with law enforcement investigations

Reporting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

How to Report

If you encounter CSAM or suspect child exploitation on The Yo:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the "Report" button on any profile, event, or content
  • Email: safety@bigvibestudios.com
  • Emergency Contact: michael@bigvibestudios.com

Our Response Process

  1. Immediate Action: Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours
  2. Content Removal: CSAM is immediately removed and reported to authorities
  3. Account Suspension: Offending accounts are permanently banned
  4. Law Enforcement Notification: We report all CSAM incidents to:
    • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
    • Appropriate law enforcement agencies
    • CyberTipline: www.missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline
  5. Evidence Preservation: All relevant data is preserved for investigation

Compliance with Child Safety Laws

The Yo complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) - United States
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union
  • UK Age Appropriate Design Code - United Kingdom
  • All regional and national child protection laws

Reporting to Authorities

We maintain relationships with and report to:

  • NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children)
  • FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)
  • Local and federal law enforcement as required by jurisdiction
  • International authorities for cross-border incidents

Community Guidelines

All users must adhere to our Community Guidelines, which prohibit:

  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Grooming or inappropriate contact with minors
  • Sharing or soliciting explicit content involving minors
  • Any behavior that endangers child safety

Violations result in immediate permanent ban and law enforcement notification.

Parental Controls & Guidance

We recommend parents and guardians:

  • Discuss online safety with their children
  • Monitor their child's app usage
  • Report any concerning behavior to us immediately
  • Review our Privacy Policy

Employee Training

All Big Vibe Studios team members receive training on:

  • Identifying CSAM and exploitation indicators
  • Proper reporting procedures
  • Legal compliance requirements
  • Trauma-informed content moderation practices

Transparency & Accountability

We are committed to transparency in our child safety efforts:

  • Regular review and updates of safety policies
  • Cooperation with safety organizations and researchers
  • Continuous improvement of detection and prevention tools
  • Public reporting of our safety efforts (annual transparency reports)
